Support

Kaldarshi is made by one person. If a date looks wrong, something is broken, or a festival is missing, write to me.

Get in touch

Email w3radar.apps@gmail.com. I read everything and usually reply within a few days.

For a problem, these help most:

  • What you did, and what happened instead of what you expected
  • Your phone and OS version, for example "iPhone 15, iOS 26" or "Pixel 9, Android 16"
  • For a wrong date: the day, your place, and the source you compared against

Sunrise and sunset look off

Times depend on the place chosen. Open the place name at the top of Today and check it is your city, or allow location so the app can find it. Times are for the centre of the place, so they can differ by a minute or two from a printed calendar for the same city.

Works offline

The calendar is saved on your device, so Kaldarshi works without internet.
